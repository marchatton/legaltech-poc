# Governance (Reviews + Audits)

This section is a home for governance artefacts that cut across projects and features:
- Architecture reviews and audits (agent-native or human)
- Security reviews / threat models (when explicitly requested)
- Process decisions that affect how we ship and verify

## What goes here
- Audit reports with dated filenames
- Follow-up checklists and remediation plans
- Links back to dossiers/PRs where work was applied

## What does not go here
- Feature briefs/PRDs (those live in the relevant dossier under `docs/04-projects/`)
- Architecture contracts (canonical in `docs/03-architecture/`)
