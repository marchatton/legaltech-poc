# Segmentation Notes (Hypotheses)

This doc captures early segmentation hypotheses for the PoC.
It should be validated with practitioner interviews and fixture-driven reality.

Reference: explicit segmentation tests (consistent needs, product-specific, targetable, prioritisable, winnable).

## Segmentation dimensions (candidate)
- Role:
  - Producer: junior associate / paralegal doing first pass extraction
  - Reviewer: senior associate / partner validating and signing off
- Deal cadence:
  - High-volume repeatable diligence vs occasional bespoke deals
- Pack quality:
  - Mostly digital PDFs vs scan-heavy/missing exhibits/rotations
- Workflow maturity:
  - Checklist/template-driven vs ad hoc

## P0 (PoC) target
- Internal demo operator + 1 friendly practitioner reviewer.
- Practitioners who value:
  - fast “first pass” artefacts
  - explicit uncertainty (“missing input” is acceptable)
  - evidence locking and verification

## P1 (next) targets
- Mid-size firms doing frequent CRE diligence with standard deliverable expectations.
- Teams already frustrated by:
  - missing exhibits
  - “breadcrumbs” across title commitment + instruments + survey

## Non-targets (for now)
- Consumer real estate.
- Users who expect the system to make legal/materiality decisions.
- Teams who only want generic chat answers without evidence contracts.

## Quick validation plan
Evidence we should gather:
- 2-4 practitioner conversations focused on:
  - “What does a usable first pass output look like?”
  - “What do you need to trust it?”
- Fixture pack alignment:
  - Which pack types are most common? (scan-heavy vs clean)
  - Which failure journeys are most important? (missing exhibits, citation mismatch)
