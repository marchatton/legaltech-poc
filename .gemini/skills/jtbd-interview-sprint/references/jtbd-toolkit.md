# JTBD toolkit

## JTBD definition
Define a JTBD as: the progress a person is trying to make in a particular struggling circumstance.

## Struggling moment
Treat struggling moments as the trigger for change (and a form of nudge).

## JTBD timeline
1) First thought
2) Passive looking
3) Active looking
4) Deciding (trade-offs + value)
5) Onboarding (meeting expectations, delivering satisfaction)
6) Ongoing use (habit)

## 4 forces
- Push of the situation (problem pressure)
- Pull of the new solution (attraction)
- Habit of the present (business as usual)
- Anxiety of the new solution (uncertainty)

## Interview principles (remember these)
Humility, Causality, Trade-offs, Disconnected, Lies

## Only interview active deciders
Purchased, churned, purchased alternative, or meaningfully tried but did not buy.
