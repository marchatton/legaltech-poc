# JTBD interview script (timeline + forces)

## Warm-up
- Role and context
- What does “a good day” look like for this job?

## Timeline
1) First thought: what created space in the brain?
2) Passive looking: what did they learn, where?
3) Active looking: what options became viable?
4) Deciding: trade-offs, what mattered, what was scary?
5) Onboarding: first experience, expectation vs reality?
6) Ongoing use: habit formation, what keeps/blocks it?

## Forces
- Push: what made the situation unacceptable?
- Pull: what attracted them to a new approach?
- Habit: what kept them doing the old thing?
- Anxiety: what uncertainty made them hesitate?

## Alternatives and trade-offs
- What else did they consider?
- What made them choose or reject each option?

## Wrap
- What would “perfect” look like?
- What would have made this 10x easier?
