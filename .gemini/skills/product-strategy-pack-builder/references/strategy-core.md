# Strategy core (internal)

## Definitions (keep clean)
- Mission: why
- Vision: what
- Strategy: how
- Segmentation: who
- Positioning: where
- Roadmap: when

## Segmentation pre-qualifiers (avoid)
Demographics, psychographics, composite personas, “crossing the chasm” buckets.

## 5 tests of an effective segmentation framework
- Consistent needs
- Product-specific
- Targetable
- Prioritisable
- Winnable

## Differentiation drivers (scan)
User needs, alternatives, unique assets, industry direction, creative choices, chosen segments.

## Product stages
Explore, expand, extract.
