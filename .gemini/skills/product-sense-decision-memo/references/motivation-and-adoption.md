# Motivation, Friction, Satisfaction, Nudges (quick reference)

## Motivation questions
- What is the job?
- How important is the job?
- How urgent is the job?
- What else is more urgent or more important?
- What are the benefits of action?
- What are the consequences of inaction?
- How good is the alternative?

## Friction checklist (scan for blockers)
- Do I understand this?
- When do I need to decide?
- How complex is the decision?
- What is the cost of the wrong decision?
- Do I understand the very next action?
- How hard is it to initiate?
- How hard do I think it is to complete?
- What else is going on in my life/work right now?
- What do I stand to lose?
- How inconsistent is this with expectations/habits/experience?
- How much thought is needed before acting?

## Satisfaction checklist (scan for follow-through)
1) Did it fulfil the promised job?
2) Did it live up to expectations?
3) Did it generate happy hormones?
4) Did it give social validation?
5) Did it feel reassuring?
6) Did it raise prestige / self-esteem / security?
7) Did it make the user feel smart?

## Adoption equation (mental model)
Nudge * (motivation - friction) ^ satisfaction = adoption

## Nudges
- Intrinsic: comes from within (habits, self-imposed rules, personal motivations)
- Extrinsic: comes from environment/context (defaults, social proof, policy, prompts)
