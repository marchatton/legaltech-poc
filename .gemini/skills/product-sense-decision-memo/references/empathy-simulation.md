# Cognitive empathy: simulation prompts

## Two questions to ask in any product situation
1) What is the user really trying to do right now?
2) What is the user feeling right now?

## Cognitive empathy components (for scoring and self-check)
- Metacognition: notice own thinking, motivations, biases
- Intellectual flexibility: update beliefs under ambiguity
- Simulation: mentally simulate how others think/feel/act given context

## Simulation procedure (fast)
- Describe the circumstance in concrete detail (time, pressure, environment, constraints)
- Describe progress desired (what “better” looks like in their words)
- List alternatives and “business-as-usual”
- Predict anxieties and reasons to stall
- Identify the one smallest step that reduces anxiety the most
