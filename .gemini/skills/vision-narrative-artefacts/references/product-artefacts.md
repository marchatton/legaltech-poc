# Product artefacts

## The “1-2-3-4” set
1) One vision
2) Two product strategy
3) Three product proposals
4) Four PRFAQs

## How to do vision + strategy properly
1) Hold a high-level vision in mind
2) Formulate a winning strategy and write it down
3) Paint the full vision as a vivid description of the main bits of the strategy and beyond

## Amazon 5Q product proposal
1) Who is the customer?
2) What is the problem/opportunity?
3) What is the main customer benefit?
4) How do you know customers want this?
5) What is the customer experience?

## Comms clarity check
Ensure messaging answers:
- What does it do?
- Is it for me?
- How good is it?
- What should I do now?
