# Thinking tools for stress-testing

## Pre-mortem
Assume the idea failed. List reasons. Convert each reason into a mitigation or a test.

## Nth order effects
List incentives created by the change. Predict what users/partners will optimise for after 2–3 iterations.

## Null hypothesis (B2B default)
Assume the customer does not need it, will not pay, and will not deploy/use it. Design to disprove.

## Six thinking hats (quick usage)
- White: facts, data
- Yellow: benefits
- Black: risks
- Red: intuition/emotion
- Green: new ideas
- Blue: process control
